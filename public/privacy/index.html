Whitelist Privacy Notice

What we collect

When you request early access, we collect only your email address, your explicit consent and the submission time. You do not create a Pentagon account by submitting this form.

How we use your information

We use the information to review requests and contact you about Pentagon early access. Requests are reviewed manually and joining the whitelist does not automatically unlock admin or dashboard access.

Retention and access

Requests are stored in a restricted, separate marketing environment. We intend to retain pending applications for up to 12 months and remove expired records during subsequent maintenance. Only authorized project operators should access the requests. We do not publish your email address.

Your choices

You can ask us to review or delete a request by contacting support@pentagon.quest. Do not submit passwords or confidential information through the whitelist form.

Service status

This is an early-access request process, not a subscription payment or guarantee of access. The marketing preview remains under review.
